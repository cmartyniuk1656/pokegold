### Celadon Move Tutor Changes That Were Made

The spreadsheet moves `FLAMETHROWER`, `ICE_BEAM`, and `THUNDERBOLT` are not TM/HM items in this repo, so they were implemented as tutor-only compatibility instead of being forced into the `tmhm` macro.

Files changed for the tutor:
- `engine/events/celadon_move_tutor.asm`: added the Celadon tutor special, move menu, compatibility check, and normal learn-move flow.
- `data/pokemon/celadon_tutor_compatibility.asm`: added generated per-species compatibility masks for the three tutor moves.
- `data/events/special_pointers.asm` and `main.asm`: registered and included the tutor special.
- `maps/CeladonDeptStore3F.asm`: added the move trainer NPC on Celadon Dept. Store 3F.

Tutor compatibility generated from the TM/HM sheet:
- Pokemon with at least one tutor-only move: 150
- `FLAMETHROWER`: 65 Pokemon
- `ICE_BEAM`: 84 Pokemon
- `THUNDERBOLT`: 68 Pokemon

### TM/HM Changes That Could Not Be Made

None. All in-scope spreadsheet TM/HM moves are now represented either in `tmhm` flags or in the Celadon tutor compatibility table. `FLAMETHROWER`, `ICE_BEAM`, and `THUNDERBOLT` remain non-TM/HM items by design.
